Why I made this

A million photos. Zero baby books.

Six years later, and every one of those photos is still on my phone. No baby book. No photo book. Because sorting through them was time-consuming. Now that my second baby is here, it’s all coming back again.

What I wanted was a baby book blended with a journal. With a baby book, you get the prompts, but you have to match them to the photos. With a photo book, you have to go through and sort it all yourself. I wanted something that could do it all for me: go through my photos, pick the best, and ask the one question that makes me remember who they were at that exact moment.

So I built it. My kiddos are getting baby books after all — one overdue, one right on time.

Zenia

FAQ

Asked, answered.

How are the scenes and questions written?

Each moment is written by AI — based on who’s in the photo, the place, the light, what’s happening. Then it asks a question specifically for you.

Do you look through all my photos?

Only during the time period you selected. The scan runs on your phone — that’s where faces are found and grouped, and your library itself is never uploaded.

Two small things do leave: face crops, so Claude can double-check the grouping, and the few photos in the running for a moment. Both go over an encrypted connection to our own server, which hands them to Claude. No photos are kept on our servers — the only thing we track is how many AI tokens each request used.

We never sell your data — not your photos, not your answers, not to advertisers, not to anyone. When you delete something, we delete it too.

How is AI used, exactly?

In a couple of ways — and your data is never sold, and never used to train AI.

  • Face scanning — finding faces and grouping them, so your people can be followed through the year. This runs on your phone; only a few crops go to Claude to confirm the groups.
  • The moments — Claude (Anthropic’s AI) looks at a handful of candidate photos, picks the ones worth writing about, and writes each scene and its question.
What if I fall behind?

A moment waits as long as it needs to, and the next one arrives on your usual day.

What does it cost?

Found & Felt is in early testing with a small group of families, free while we learn. Downloading a moment as a PDF is always free. Printing isn’t open yet — when it is, the price will be shown before you order, and nothing goes to press without you saying so.
