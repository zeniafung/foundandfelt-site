5. How photos are handled

  • Written moments are generated with the help of Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. Content sent to Anthropic through their API is not used to train their models.
  • You can stop giving the app access to your photos at any time in your phone’s settings.

You can edit or delete any moment, and we’d encourage you to read anything before it’s printed.

Your answers are your own writing and are never generated for you.
